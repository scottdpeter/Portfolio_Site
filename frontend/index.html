About Me

I'm a software engineer who spent years as a critical care nurse. That experience taught me where healthcare systems actually break down — and why building better tools matters. I'm focused on data interoperability, clinical workflows, and backend systems that work in the real world.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me with family, woodworking, or enjoying Colorado sunshine. I'm passionate about using software to help my community, and I bring the same thoughtful, detail-oriented mindset from nursing into everything I build.

Healthcare Interoperability

Epic-to-LIS HL7 Interface Design

Production-modeled HL7 v2.5.1 ADT^A01 interface transmitting patient admission data from Epic to a Laboratory Information System via MLLP over TCP/IP. Includes full segment-level data mapping (MSH, EVN, PID, PV1, GT1, IN1), PHI handling with SSN masking, and a comprehensive error architecture — AE/AR ACK handling, FIFO retry queues, duplicate detection, and volume anomaly alerting.
